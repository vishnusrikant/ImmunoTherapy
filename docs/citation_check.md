## Most important problems

1. **NLR -> irAEs is cited to a paper that never studies NLR** (line 102). Fang et al. 2025 has no NLR analysis. Remove the citation. This matters because NLR is the project's main available lab feature.
2. **SCORPIO is mis-used as CRP/irAE evidence** (line 222). SCORPIO predicts efficacy (survival and benefit), has no irAE outcome, and uses no CRP. It is also 2025, not 2024.
3. **The IL-6 claim is overstated twice** (lines 94, 223). The Bone Marrow Transplant 2025 paper (n=101) uses IL-6 at day 3 in an any-grade ICANS model only. It says nothing about predicting severe CRS.
4. **The AUC 0.99 "best published result" is misleading** (lines 293, BG121). It is a 34-patient pilot detecting irAEs from on-treatment symptom reports. Larger EHR studies get AUC ~0.70-0.76.
5. **Chowell cohort described wrongly** (line 174, BG68). It is one MSK cohort split at random 80/20, not multi-institution training plus an MSK held-out set.
6. **JCO CCI citations are mixed up** (lines 265, 339). The "90-day acute care" study is Lu et al. 2023 (PMID 37001039), which is missing from Sources. The linked paper is a different study (Lippenszky 2024), and its year is listed as 2023.
7. **Wrong years / venue details**: ASH abstract is Blood 2025, not 2024 (lines 93, 340). SCORPIO is Nat Med 2025 (line 347). The npj "85.97M claims" are enrollees; 92,858 patients were analysed (line 92).
8. **Unsupported claims**: the "~70% to ~90%" combination-therapy figure could not be found in the ASCO guideline (full text blocked). "Family history ... several studies" (line 225) has no source and none was found. Khan 2025 is not the "first" cytokine + irAE cohort (BG70).
9. **Framing**: the age and autoimmune odds ratios are correct, but they come from one single-centre retrospective study. The heading "validated in multiple studies" (line 85) overstates this.

Not checked (no citation attached): the organ-incidence table (lines 32-41), the CAR-T incidence ranges (lines 51-54), and the executive-summary "70-90%" / "50-93%" figures (line 5, BG9, BG43). These need sources. For comparison, the cited Fang 2025 cohort itself reports 29.0% any-grade and 4.6% grade >=3 irAEs.
